📦 5 Stress-Free Moving Tips

1. The “Security First” Inventory
Before packing, create a digital inventory. Photograph high-value items and serial numbers for insurance purposes.

2. Strategy Over Speed: Start Early
Begin 4-6 weeks in advance. Declutter into "keep," "donate," "discard" piles to reduce workload and cost.

3. Pack an “Essentials Vault”
Pack a bag with documents, chargers, toiletries, and a change of clothes for the first 24 hours. Keep it with you.

4. Smart Labeling & Room Assignment
Label boxes on three sides with contents and room. Colour-code for efficient placement.

5. Interprovincial Insurance Update
Notify your insurer when crossing provinces to update coverage and confirm goods-in-transit protection.

📦 Professional Packing Guide

1. The "Double-Wrap" Protocol
Layer 1: industrial plastic wrap seals out dust. Layer 2: premium bubble wrap secured with tape. For hollow items, stuff interior with crumpled paper.

2. Vertical is Vital (The "Record Player" Rule)
Pack plates on their edges like vinyl records. Stemware should be upright, never on sides.

3. Stop The Glitches
Line box bottoms with bubble wrap. Fill every gap with plastic wrap or soft items. No rattling allowed.

4. Box Integrity
Heavy items in small reinforced boxes. Seal bottom seams with an "H" pattern of high-strength tape.

5. Clear Signs for the Crew
Mark "FRAGILE" and "THIS SIDE UP" with arrows on at least two sides of each box.
